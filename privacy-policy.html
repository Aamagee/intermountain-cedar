Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Information We Collect

When you submit the quote request form on this website, we collect the information you provide: your name, phone number, email address, project location, project type, estimated budget, desired timeline, and project description. We do not collect this information anywhere else on the site.

How Your Information Is Used

Your quote request opens a pre-filled email in your own email application, addressed to us. That message is sent directly from your device to our inbox using your own email account — this website does not store your submission on a server or database. We use the information solely to respond to your inquiry and discuss your project.

Cookies & Analytics

This website does not currently use cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics services. If that changes in the future, this policy will be updated to reflect it.

Third-Party Links

This site links to third-party sites we're affiliated with, such as Nico Secure Storage. Those sites have their own privacy practices, and we encourage you to review their policies separately.

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes. Information submitted through the contact form is used only to respond to your quote request.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, you can reach us by phone at (435) 671-3136 or through the contact page.

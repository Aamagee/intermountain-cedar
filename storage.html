About the Facility

Safe, clean, and easily accessible storage

Nico Secure Storage is a self storage facility in Heber City, Utah, owned by Nephi Trunnell. The facility offers clean, secure, ready-to-rent units in both standard and climate-controlled options, with temperature regulation to help protect sensitive items from Utah's seasonal weather extremes.

Units can be rented and managed online, with account access available any time.

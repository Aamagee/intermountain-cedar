Meet Our General Contractor

Nephi Trunnell

Nephi Trunnell is a hardworking Utah local with over 25 years of experience in the construction business, and serves as the general contractor behind Intermountain Cedar. The company has a history of residential construction work throughout Utah, with projects in areas including Heber City, Midway, and Park City.

Nephi stays hands-on through every stage of a project, from initial planning and framing through the final walkthrough, working to make sure each home is built the right way the first time.
